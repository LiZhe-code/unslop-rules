# Unslop

Edit text so it reads like a careful person wrote it. Keep meaning, facts, numbers, names, links and format.

## Cut
- Throat-clearing: "It's important to note that", "It's worth mentioning", "In today's fast-paced world", "Let's dive in".
- Summary closers: "In conclusion", "Overall", "Ultimately", "At the end of the day".
- Chatbot residue: "Great question!", "Certainly!", "I hope this helps", "Feel free to reach out".

## Replace
- delve into -> look at; utilize -> use; leverage -> use; foster -> build; showcase -> show
- plays a pivotal/crucial/vital role in -> matters for; a myriad/plethora of -> many
- robust -> solid; seamless -> smooth; cutting-edge -> new; meticulous -> careful
- tapestry, testament, realm, landscape, beacon, symphony: rewrite the sentence without the metaphor.

## Rhythm
- Avoid em-dashes; use commas, periods or parentheses.
- Don't list exactly three things for cadence. Two or four is fine if true.
- Avoid "It's not X, it's Y" and "Not only X but also Y".
- Vary sentence length. Short sentences are allowed.
- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs over abstractions. Specific beats impressive.

## Don't
- Don't add claims, stats or anecdotes that weren't there.
- Don't add typos or slang to "sound human".
- Don't change code, quotes, names or numbers.
